Synthesis

The Synthesis page defines the noise of up to eight simulated clocks, plots the stability that each noise model predicts, and simulates the clocks. It answers what a clock of a given noise looks like in a deviation, and it makes records with known noise for trying estimators and time scales.

The noise of a clock

A clock is the sum of up to four noises. Each has two fields, which hold the same level in two forms: the coefficient of the clock model and the coefficient $h_α$ of the power law $S_y(f) = h_α f^α$. Typing one fills in the other. A level of 0 leaves the noise out.

NoiseModelUnitPower law
White PM$R$s²$h_2 = 8π^2τ_0 R$
White FM$q_1$s$h_0 = 2 q_1$
Random-walk FM$q_2$1/s$h_{-2} = q_2/(2π^2)$
Random-run FM$q_3$1/s³$h_{-4} = q_3/(8π^4)$

The conversions are those of ClockModel. $R$ is the variance of the white PM of one phase sample, so the $h_2$ that goes with it changes with the sample interval. The page offers no flicker noise. noise_gen simulates it in Julia.

Add a clock adds a clock with the noise of the last one, and Remove takes one out. The name of a clock is the field beside its color.

Predicted stability

The plot shows the deviation that each model predicts, at the averaging times of the octave grid for a record of Samples samples at the sample interval τ₀. Preview chooses MDEV, ADEV or MHDEV. The curves are computed in closed form, as powerlaw_sigma does, and they follow each value as it is entered. Nothing is simulated for them.

ADEV and MDEV do not converge for random-run FM. A clock that has it is drawn in MHDEV alone.

Generating the clocks

Generate simulates the clocks with Samples, τ₀ and Seed, as simulate_clock and simulate_ensemble do. The same seed gives the same records. Several clocks are simulated against one ideal reference, as columns that share their instants.

The clocks are added to those already loaded. One clock opens on the Data page, and two or more open on the Ensemble page, which forms a time scale from them. Run analysis then analyzes them like any other record.
