Live

The Live page follows a measurement while it runs. It reads one reading at a time from an instrument or from a simulator and updates the deviations with each one, so that the curve fills in toward long averaging times as the record grows.

The source

SourceReads fromFields
SimulatorA simulated clock with white PM, white FM and random-walk FM, at one reading per sample intervalNone
NetworkThe SCPI socket of an instrument on the network, as an LXI instrument offers, on port 5025 as a ruleAddress, as host:port, and Query
Serial / fileA serial port, or a file of readings, which is read to its end at oncePath and Query

The Query is sent before every reading. For the network it is READ? at first. Leave it empty for an instrument that sends readings by itself.

A serial port has to be set up before the measurement starts, since the explorer opens it as a file. On Linux, stty -F /dev/ttyUSB0 9600 raw sets the line speed, and on macOS stty -f does.

From a browser, the serial port and the file are those of the computer that runs serve.py, which serves them only on its default address.

The readings

A reading is the first number on a line. Other fields on the line, after a comma, a semicolon or a blank, are ignored.

SettingMeaning
ReadingsCounter frequency (Hz), Fractional frequency or Phase (s). The simulator gives phase
Nominal (Hz)The frequency that counter readings are measured against. Left empty, it is the first reading rounded to four significant digits
τ₀ (s)The time from one reading to the next, such as the gate time of the counter
EstimatorsAny of ADEV, MDEV, HDEV and MHDEV

A counter reading $ν$ becomes the fractional frequency $y = ν/ν_0 - 1$ against the nominal frequency $ν_0$. A nominal value that is off by a little adds a constant frequency offset, which none of the four deviations responds to.

The sample interval is not measured: set it to the time from one reading to the next. Frequency readings are taken as averages over that whole time. Dead time between the readings is not corrected for (Counter readings).

A measurement cannot skip a reading. When an instrument answers with the SCPI value for no measurement, 9.91E+37, or with a line that does not start with a number, the measurement stops with that as its error.

While it runs

Start begins the measurement and Stop ends it. The bar at the top gives the state, the number of readings, the time they span and the last reading. The two plots are updated about four times a second:

PlotShows
σ(τ) so farThe chosen deviations of all the readings so far, on the octave grid
Fractional frequencyThe fractional frequency of the last 5000 readings

The deviations are those of adev, mdev, hdev and mhdev on the readings so far, updated with running sums as StreamingStability keeps them (Dobrogowski and Kasznia (2007)). They carry no confidence intervals and no noise types. Hovering over a point shows the number of terms behind it. The points at the longest averaging times rest on few terms and move the most.

Afterwards

ButtonAction
Save readingsSaves the readings as a text file, one per line, under a line that states the record type, the sample interval and the source. Counter readings are saved as fractional frequency
Analyse readingsLoads the readings as a new clock, opens the Stability page and runs the analysis, with confidence intervals, noise types and every estimator of the list. It needs a stopped measurement of 16 readings or more
