Bibliography

The sources that the documentation and the docstrings cite, in alphabetical order. A citation in the text links to its entry here and names the section, the equation or the table that supports the statement. Each entry gives a DOI or a public address where one exists. The papers and standards themselves are not distributed with the package.

Three general works are listed for further reading: Sullivan et al. (1990), a volume of reprinted papers on the characterization of clocks and oscillators, Allan et al. (1997), an introduction to timekeeping, and Banerjee and Matsakis (2023), a textbook on timekeeping and time transfer.
