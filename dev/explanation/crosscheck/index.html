Conventions of the rows

  • Bias correction. allantools returns the total deviations and Thêo1 uncorrected, so its rows call totdev, mtotdev, ttotdev, htotdev and theo1 with correct_bias=false. Stable32 corrects the total and the Hadamard total deviation on FM noise and prints the modified total and time total deviations uncorrected. Its total and Hadamard total rows take the uncorrected value of this package with Stable32's factor for the noise type Stable32 printed, so that they compare the estimator and not the noise identification. Its modified total and time total rows use correct_bias=false.
  • Noise type. The Stable32 rows of the Hadamard deviations use remove_drift=false, which identifies the noise on the record as given, as Stable32 does. The rows "Stable32 CI (S32 α)" compute the interval for the noise type Stable32 printed, which separates the EDF model from the noise identification.
  • Thêo1 averaging time. allantools reports Thêo1 at τ = m·τ0, this package and Stable32 at τ = 0.75·m·τ0. Rows are matched on m.
  • Thêo1 bias. The theobr rows compare the Thêo1 run that Stable32 makes with automatic noise identification, which removes the bias, with theo1 under correct_bias=:stable32. The theo1 rows compare its runs made for white FM, where the bias factor is 1, with correct_bias=false.
  • Welch spectra. The rows compare psd_y with scipy.signal.welch from the second bin above zero. In the bins below it scipy removes the plain mean of each segment and this package the taper-weighted mean. psd_x is psd_y divided by the response of the first difference and has no rows of its own.
  • Record length in the EDF of the total deviations. SP1065 Tables 7 and 8 give edf = b·T/τ − c. allantools takes T/τ = N/m and this package (N − 1)/m, a relative difference of 1/N. The EDF rows compare the formula at the T of allantools.
  • Time total deviation. In allantools it is the modified total deviation times τ/√3, so the generator derives it from that run.
  • MTIE. The rows compare allantools' mtie. Stable32's "MTIE (Fast)" agrees within 4e-5 at each τ it reports.
  • 1000-point test suite (SP1065 §12.4). The overlapping Allan, modified Allan, time, overlapping Hadamard, total and Hadamard total values are those of Table 31. Its Hadamard total values carry the white FM factor (B = 0.995) above m = 1. For the modified total and time total deviations Table 31 lists corrected values, the uncorrected ones divided by √0.73. Their rows compare the uncorrected values of Stable32 1.60, and the Thêo1 rows Stable32's uncorrected Thêo1, as the allantools test for this record gives them. The parabolic deviation values are from the same test, computed there with the PDev program of SigmaTheta 3.0 (October 2023).

Not compared

  • mhdev, htdev, mhtotdev and ncornered_hat: none of the programs above computes them. They are tested in test/stab/ against direct evaluation of their definitions and against identities.
  • ThêoH, and ThêoBR under correct_bias=:published. No program above measures the bias ratio up to a tenth of the record (n = ⌊N/30 − 3⌋, Howe 2006, Eq. (6)). By default this package measures it up to half the record (n = ⌊N/6 − 3⌋, SP1065 Eq. (33)), as Stable32 does. The two ratios, in variance, are 1.086 and 0.602 on the test suite, a white FM record, and 0.355 and 2.05 on the 8192-point record, where PM noise gives way to FM noise. Stable32's values are compared with correct_bias=:stable32, which takes its ratio from at most 3000 points as Stable32 does: the theobr rows above, and in test/stab/stable32gen.jl the two Thêo1 points of its ThêoH run on the 8192-point record, which the default matches within 2 %.
