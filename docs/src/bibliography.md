# Bibliography

The sources that the documentation and the docstrings cite, in
alphabetical order. A citation in the text links to its entry here and
names the section, the equation or the table that supports the statement.
Each entry gives a DOI or a public address where one exists. The papers
and standards themselves are not distributed with the package.

Three general works are listed for further reading:
[Sullivan et al. (1990)](@cite sullivan-1990-tn1337), a volume of
reprinted papers on the characterization of clocks and oscillators,
[Allan et al. (1997)](@cite allan-1997-hp-app1289), an introduction to
timekeeping, and
[Banerjee and Matsakis (2023)](@cite banerjee-2023-timekeeping), a
textbook on timekeeping and time transfer.
