Conventions the rows encode

  • Bias corrections. allantools reports every total estimator raw, so the allantools rows call totdev, mtotdev, ttotdev and htotdev with correct_bias=false. Stable32 bias-corrects TOTDEV and HTOTDEV and reports MTOT/TTOT raw; the Stable32 rows use SigmaTau's defaults for the former and correct_bias=false for the latter.
  • Thêo1 τ. allantools reports Thêo1 at τ = m·τ0, SigmaTau and Stable32 at the effective τ = 0.75·m·τ0. Rows are matched on m.
  • TOTDEV / MTOT EDF record length. SP1065 Tables 7–8 give edf = b·T/τ − c. allantools uses T/τ = N/m, SigmaTau the span (N − 1)·τ0/τ, a 1/N relative difference; the EDF rows compare the formula at allantools' T.
  • TTOTDEV in allantools is exactly MTOTDEV·τ/√3, so the generator derives it from the MTOT run instead of a second O(N·m) pass.
  • MTIE: allantools' mtie (rolling window) is compared; Stable32's "MTIE (Fast)" agrees to 4e-5 on every τ it reports.
  • NBS 1000-point values. MTOT/TTOT are Stable32 1.60's raw run; SP1065 Table 31's 2.418528e-01 is Stable32 1.53's MTOT algorithm, which neither current program reproduces. The published HTOTDEV values carry the white-FM TotHvar bias (B = 0.995) above m = 1.

Not covered

  • Non-overlapped ADEV and HDEV (allantools adev/hdev, Stable32 "Allan"/"Hadamard"): SigmaTau implements only the overlapped forms.
  • MHDEV, HTDEV, MHTOTDEV, ThêoH, NCH: no reference implements them; they are tested against inlined kernels and identities in test/stab/.
